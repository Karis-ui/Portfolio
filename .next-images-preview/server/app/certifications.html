Offensive security

OSCP

What it is: Offensive Security Certified Professional, a practical exam involving lab machines and a professional report.

What it shows: Enumeration, exploitation, privilege escalation and clear reporting under time pressure.

Networking

Cisco Networking Essentials

What it is: Foundations of how networks move traffic: addressing, routing and switching.

What it shows: Network fundamentals for interpreting packet captures and service scans.
